# Application mappings

Do not optimize for applications until the abstract mechanism survives strong controls.

## Strong structural candidates

### Contested RF / adaptive jamming
Traffic concentration can reveal valuable links; a jammer can preferentially disrupt those links. The relevant transferable property would be distributed route dispersion with graceful degradation, not "Physarum" by itself.

### P2P eclipse / peer-view manipulation
An attacker can bias or replace a node's reachable peer neighborhood. Candidate transferable properties: diversity maintenance, local memory, and resistance to targeted neighborhood shaping.

### Censorship circumvention
Relays/transports can be detected and blocked after observed use. Any contribution must be a specific resource-allocation advantage over existing adaptive/decentralized systems.

### Moving-target defense vs lateral movement
Often the dual game: the defender edits topology/configuration to disrupt an adaptive navigator. The same simulator may become useful by swapping which side is optimized.

## Weak mappings
Drug resistance and market routing may share an adaptive-opponent shape but are metaphorical until a domain-specific model is supplied.
